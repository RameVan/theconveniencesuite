  • 20+ consent platforms
  • Refuses legitimate interest

How to install
  1. Unzip the download somewhere it can stay.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin it to see the ✓ badge whenever it handles a banner.

PantryPip

A food assistant that keeps your pantry up to date with almost no effort. Snap a receipt, say "I finished the milk", tap "Cooked it". It plans meals, writes the shopping list and learns how fast you use things.

  • Receipt scanning
  • Smart shopping list
  • Works offline as a PWA
v0.1.0 · EN / NL
Try it Android
Install it on your phone or computer

Open Try it, create an account, then:

  1. iPhone / iPad: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: download the Android app and open it (allow installing from your browser when asked), or in Chrome tap ⋮ → Install app.
  3. Windows / Mac: in Chrome or Edge click the install icon in the address bar.

It opens full screen with its own icon. This is a shared test copy: your pantry is private to your account, but don't store anything you'd mind losing.

Run your own copy
  1. Install Node.js 24 or newer, then download and unzip the source.
  2. In that folder run npm install, then npm run build.
  3. Run npm start and open http://127.0.0.1:8787.
  4. For real use start with SEED_DEMO=0 to skip the demo household. On iPhone, see docs/IPHONE.md and add it to your home screen.

Opt Out

Clears the newsletters out of your Gmail. It finds every mailing list you're on, sorts them by company and type, and unsubscribes in bulk while keeping banking, orders and other important mail safe. It also tells you which senders have had a data breach.

  • Bulk unsubscribe
  • Protects important mail
  • Breach alerts
v0.1.7 · Windows 10 / 11 · 113 MB
Download

In testing: open to approved people only. Ask for access from the app's sign-in screen. Privacy policy.

How to start it
  1. Download OptOut-0.1.7.exe and double-click it. Nothing is installed.
  2. Windows may say Windows protected your PC (the app isn't code-signed yet): choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. It opens in your browser. Sign in with Google and connect your Gmail; the scan keeps running from the tray icon.
  4. New versions arrive by themselves: the tray icon offers Restart to update.

In development · Rev A prototype

TwelveVolt

A smart fuse board: a compact 12 V fuse box for a camper van that you wire like an ordinary blade-fuse box. The difference: every channel measures its own current, voltage, power and energy, and can be switched on and off from Home Assistant.

  • 16 channels
  • ±1 % measurement per channel
  • ESPHome · Home Assistant
  • 3D-printed enclosure
  • Channels15 identical channels on the board, each taking an ATO blade fuse up to 30 A. Channel 1 is a separate high-current channel for one device up to 80 A.
  • MeasuringEach channel has its own shunt and measuring chip, designed for ±1 % current and voltage readings.
  • SwitchingA smart high-side switch per channel with short-circuit and over-temperature shut-off. A jumper sets whether a channel starts on or off.
  • ControlAn ESP32-S3 on the board runs ESPHome, so Home Assistant sees and switches every channel directly.
  • EnclosurePrinted in ASA, about 296 × 212 × 53 mm.
Rev A · board SDU-15 · not yet built

Rev A exists only as a design and calculations. No board has been built, powered or tested yet, so none of the figures above have been measured. It will stay a prototype until a unit has been built and tested in the van.
