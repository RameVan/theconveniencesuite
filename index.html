A food assistant that keeps your pantry up to date with almost no effort. Snap a receipt, say "I finished the milk", tap "Cooked it". It plans meals, writes the shopping list and learns how fast you use things.

  • Receipt scanning
  • Smart shopping list

Try it Android
Install it on your phone or computer

Open Try it, create an account, then:

  1. iPhone / iPad: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: download the Android app and open it (allow installing from your browser when asked), or in Chrome tap ⋮ → Install app.
  3. Windows / Mac: in Chrome or Edge click the install icon in the address bar.

It opens full screen with its own icon. This is a shared test copy: your pantry is private to your account, but don't store anything you'd mind losing.

Run your own copy
  1. Install Node.js 24 or newer, then download and unzip the source.
  2. In that folder run npm install, then npm run build.
  3. Run npm start and open http://127.0.0.1:8787.
  4. For real use start with SEED_DEMO=0 to skip the demo household. On iPhone, see docs/IPHONE.md and add it to your home screen.

In development · Rev A prototype

TwelveVolt

A smart fuse board: a compact 12 V fuse box for a camper van that you wire like an ordinary blade-fuse box. The difference: every channel measures its own current, voltage, power and energy, and can be switched on and off from Home Assistant.

  • 16 channels
  • ±1 % measurement per channel
  • ESPHome · Home Assistant
  • 3D-printed enclosure
  • Channels15 identical channels on the board, each taking an ATO blade fuse up to 30 A. Channel 1 is a separate high-current channel for one device up to 80 A.
  • MeasuringEach channel has its own shunt and measuring chip, designed for ±1 % current and voltage readings.
  • SwitchingA smart high-side switch per channel with short-circuit and over-temperature shut-off. A jumper sets whether a channel starts on or off.
  • ControlAn ESP32-S3 on the board runs ESPHome, so Home Assistant sees and switches every channel directly.
  • EnclosurePrinted in ASA, about 296 × 212 × 53 mm.

TwelveVolt Modular

TwelveVolt cut into slices. A row head holds the smart parts, and you add channels two at a time: each module slides onto the end of the row, connects power and signals as it slides in, and is held to the wall by two screws. The row head finds every module and its place in the row by itself.

  • Slide-in modules
  • 2 channels per module
  • Modules found automatically
  • ESPHome · Home Assistant
  • Rev A rowA row head, one TwelveVolt SM, one TwelveVolt M and one TwelveVolt M+: about 202 mm wide.
  • PowerEach module carries its own piece of copper bus, with a pin on the left and a spring socket on the right. Only the socket side is ever live, so the open end of a row is safe to touch. The row is fed through an 80 A MEGA fuse; all its regular channels together are designed for up to 64 A continuous.
  • SafetyFuses and smart switches act on their own module. If the row head fails, outputs hold, and a jumper per channel sets the power-up state.
  • HeaterAn optional channel setting for a diesel heater: switching it off only cuts power once the heater has drawn almost nothing for 5 minutes, so it can always finish its cooldown.
  • HousingsPrinted in ASA and screwed to the wall, with a hinged cap over every output terminal and the module type engraved in each cover.

All four circuit boards are designed and routed, and the housings are modelled for a first test print. Nothing has been ordered, built, powered or tested yet, so none of the figures here have been measured.

The modules

  • 76 mm Row head Feed + core · TVM-CORE

    The start of every row. An ESP32-S3 running ESPHome reports to Home Assistant and finds each module and its position. It has a watchdog, USB-C for setup and status LEDs. The row's feed cable lands on an M8 bolt in the same housing.

  • 36 mm TwelveVolt SM Switch + measure · TVM-REG

    Two channels back to back. Each has an ATO blade fuse up to 30 A, a 1 mΩ shunt with an INA226 measuring chip, and a BTS50015 smart switch with short-circuit and over-temperature shut-off. The module has its own temperature sensor and remembers its type and calibration.

  • 36 mm TwelveVolt M Measure only · TVM-MEAS

    The same size, fuses and measuring as the SM, without the switches: fuse, shunt, output, always on. For loads that software should never switch off, such as a diesel heater. It needs 46 parts instead of 73.

  • 54 mm TwelveVolt M+ 80 A, measure only · TVM-HP

    For one large load up to 80 A, such as an air conditioner. It has its own 80 A MEGA feed, and the current runs through bolted M8 studs and a busbar shunt, never across a circuit board. An INA228 measures it, and it keeps measuring without the row head.
